# Canonical Documents Summary

This is a plain-language guide for teammates writing the SRS. [Canonical-Documents.md](./Canonical-Documents.md) is the knowledge base's technical catalog of the same five sources; this file instead explains, in everyday terms, when to open the full document rather than rely on a short description.

## CLINIQ Project Plan

The [Project Plan](./Canonical-Documents/CLINIQ_Project_Plan.md) explains the clinic's problem, goals, scope, planned system, timeline, budget, risks, and the reasons the project exists. Open the full plan when the SRS needs an official project objective, scope boundary, resource limit, risk, or schedule and budget context.

## CLINIQ Modules and Features

[Modules and Features](./Canonical-Documents/CLINIQ_Modules_and_Features.md) lists what each clinic area does, who may use it, and which work is deliberately deferred. Open it whenever the SRS needs exact functions, role permissions, workflow details, or a clear in-scope versus future feature decision.

## CLINIQ Frontend Context Brief

The [Frontend Context Brief](./Canonical-Documents/CLINIQ_Frontend_Context_Brief.md) describes the intended screens, users, information, interactions, privacy rules, and broad interface expectations. Open the full brief when writing detailed user-facing behavior, screen requirements, role restrictions, or accessibility expectations.

## CLINIQ Frontend Design Reference

The [Frontend Design Reference](./Canonical-Documents/CLINIQ_Frontend_Design_Reference.md) shows the visual rules and five representative screens that establish the design patterns for the rest of the system. Open it when the SRS needs precise interface presentation, feedback states, responsive behavior, or consistency guidance for a screen.

## CLINIQ Review of Related Systems

The [Review of Related Systems](./Canonical-Documents/CLINIQ_Review_of_Related_Systems.md) compares CLINIQ with ten existing systems and records the research that informed the selected features and exclusions. Open the full review when the SRS needs comparison evidence, source citations, or an explanation of why CLINIQ includes or leaves out a capability.
